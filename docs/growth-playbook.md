# DealScout growth playbook

## Positioning

DealScout at Dealscouted.com helps Amazon shoppers browse offers by department, compare checked prices against listing reference prices, and keep a shortlist. Avoid claiming historical lows, hands-on testing, guaranteed savings, or a large audience without evidence.

Keep department browsing central. Articles belong under the footer link. Start promotion with Electronics and Home & Kitchen, linking to a stocked department or a useful comparison tool rather than an empty collection.

## Ready-to-review founder introduction

I built DealScout to make Amazon deal browsing less noisy. Offers are organized by department, with recently checked prices and clear reference-price savings. You can save finds to revisit or follow new deals in an RSS reader. I run the site, and it earns affiliate commissions from qualifying purchases. I’d appreciate feedback on whether the department browsing and comparisons help you find useful offers: https://dealscouted.com/

## Useful-resource introduction

Comparing two Amazon offers? This checklist includes a delivered-cost calculator that accounts for quantities, shipping, tax and eligible discounts you enter. It makes the calculation transparent rather than assuming the biggest discount percentage wins. I run the site, which uses affiliate links: https://dealscouted.com/guides/amazon-deal-checklist

## Promotion sequence

1. Choose a small existing audience or a community that explicitly permits founder introductions or useful-resource posts. Check its current rules before posting. Submit one tailored introduction, identify the connection to the site, and answer feedback. Posting destinations and accounts must be selected before publication; these drafts have not been sent.
2. Show one genuinely useful comparison or currently verified find. Use the public product URL and confirm it still opens before sharing. Do not reuse old prices as current offers.
3. Give interested readers the follow page: https://dealscouted.com/follow. RSS supports returning readers without a new mailing platform; the saved list is a separate device-based shortlist.
4. Convert repeated questions into focused article improvements. Prefer an original worked example, calculator or comparison checklist to multiple near-duplicate keyword pages.
5. Review results weekly before expanding promotion. Keep a small log of the destination, date, link used, replies and concrete feedback. Do not add recipients to mailing lists without their request.

## Measurement

Use Search Console’s last settled reporting period. Track branded versus non-branded impressions, clicks, indexed core pages and useful query/page combinations. An accepted sitemap is not proof of indexing. Compare periods consistently and distinguish new deployments from Google’s later crawl dates.

The site currently has no linked GA4 property in GSC Wizard, so Search Console cannot establish referral traffic, repeat visits or conversion rates. Choose and verify a traffic-measurement setup before claiming those results. Do not invent a conversion rate from search clicks.

The scheduled crawl check covers the homepage, articles, follow page, feed and configured deal collections. RSS and sharing reuse the approved public catalog and do not require additional Rainforest discovery calls. Preserve the ordinary daily allowance, enabled Prime Day allowance, and absolute monthly cap.
